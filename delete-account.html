Delete Account

Request deletion of your TONIGHT account even if you no longer have the app installed.

Not ready for public launch.

This is a working draft. Confirm the legal operator, exact data practices, retention periods and applicable law before relying on this page in app-store submissions.

Operator

Account deletion requests are handled by Braescu Robert Gabriel, operator of TONIGHT.

Option 1 — In the TONIGHT app

Open Profile → Delete Account and follow the on-screen confirmation process. This is the built-in deletion route.

Option 2 — From this website

Use the button below to open an email addressed to TONIGHT Support. Include the email used for your TONIGHT account; do not include passwords or verification codes. If the button does not work, email robertgaby13@icloud.com with subject “TONIGHT account deletion request”.

What happens next

Support will review the request, may verify account ownership using a safe method, and will communicate the steps and outcome. The email button only starts a request; it does not automatically delete data. A published version must state a reliable response process and any legally justified retention categories or periods.

Data involved

An account-deletion request concerns the account and associated profile, matching, messages, media, attendance and notification data, subject to verified technical deletion flows and any lawful retention exceptions. Data deletion must be tested before release.
