Privacy Policy

How TONIGHT expects to handle personal information while supporting dating, friendship, messaging and events.

Not ready for public launch.

This is a working draft. Confirm the legal operator, exact data practices, retention periods and applicable law before relying on this page in app-store submissions.

Who operates TONIGHT

To complete before publication: Data controller/operator: Braescu Robert Gabriel, acting as an individual in the Netherlands. A valid contact address and any additional required identification details must be confirmed before final publication. Contact: robertgaby13@icloud.com.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, TONIGHT may process account email, profile name, declared age, city, gender and preferences, photos, plans, likes, matches, messages, audio messages, reports, blocks and event attendance indicators (Interested / Going).

Technical and communication data

The app uses authentication/session identifiers, device push tokens, notification records, and technical information necessary to deliver calls and messages. Voice/video calling may involve audio/video streams and connection metadata. Precise device location should only be included in this disclosure if actually collected; the map may instead rely on event/club coordinates and a selected city.

Why information is used

To create accounts, display profiles, provide matching and chat, enable audio/video calls, show clubs and events, send requested notifications, detect abuse, and answer privacy/support requests. The applicable GDPR legal basis for each purpose must be documented and validated before release.

Service providers

Current implementation uses Supabase (authentication, database, storage and server functions), LiveKit (real-time calling), Google Maps (maps), and Ticketmaster (event listings and ticket links). Confirm all relevant providers, SDKs, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, and their roles before publication.

Sharing and visibility

Other app users can see profile information and participation indicators exposed through TONIGHT features. Chat content is intended for conversation participants, subject to applicable safety/admin processes. Event ticket links may open external services subject to their own policies.

Retention and deletion

To complete before publication: Define retention periods for each data category, including messages, voice recordings, logs, reports, and backups. Users can access Delete Account in the app or submit a request using the public deletion page. Verify that deletion actually removes associated data across database tables, storage, external systems and backups in line with applicable requirements. Explain any justified retention exceptions.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing, and may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. Contact us by email for privacy requests.

Security, age and changes

TONIGHT is intended for adults 18 and older. The app has reporting and blocking features. No service can promise absolute security. Material updates to this policy should be dated and communicated appropriately.
