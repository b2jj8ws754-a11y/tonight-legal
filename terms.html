Terms of Use

Rules for using TONIGHT responsibly and interacting with other people and event listings.

Not ready for public launch.

Operator and acceptance

To complete before publication: Insert the legally responsible operator identity, address, effective date and jurisdiction-specific terms. These draft terms do not replace legal review. Using the app will be subject to final terms made available to users.

Adults only

TONIGHT is intended for people aged 18 or older. Users must provide accurate account information and must not impersonate anyone else.

Respect and safety

Harassment, threats, hate, coercion, scams, exploitation, non-consensual sexual content and misuse of other people’s personal information are prohibited. Users can block or report inappropriate accounts or content. TONIGHT may restrict accounts or review reports as permitted by law and disclosed policies.

Meeting in person

Users choose whether to meet. Exercise caution when meeting new people, use public locations where appropriate, and never assume that an “I’m Going” indicator proves someone owns a ticket or will attend.

Events and external tickets

Event descriptions, prices, dates and availability may be supplied by third parties and can change. “Interested” and “I’m Going” reflect user intentions only. Ticket purchases occur on external websites subject to those providers’ terms.

User content and permissions

Users are responsible for content they submit and must have the rights to share it. The precise content-license scope, reporting process, moderation procedure and appeal mechanism must be documented and reviewed before release.

Account closure

Account deletion can be initiated in the app. A separate web deletion request page is available to users who cannot access the app. Disclosures about any legally required retention must match the final Privacy Policy.

Law, liability and changes

To complete before publication: Have a qualified reviewer assess consumer rights, limitation-of-liability language, governing law, disputes and any local requirements in countries where TONIGHT is offered.
